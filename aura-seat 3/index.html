Aura Seat

London · Chairs and nothing else

A concept store in London that sells chairs and nothing else. The whole system is one move: the frame of the chair is the letter A. Five carousels, each with its own colour mood — cream, black, paper, red, and a closing set where the grounds take turns.

01

The mark

Cream — the daylight set · post-01-the-mark

Aura Seat is a concept store in London that sells chairs and nothing else.

The identity is one move: the frame of the chair is the letter A. Black frame, red seat, and neither half works alone. The card is where it gets physical — letterpressed into thick cotton stock with the cut edge painted red, so the accent colour turns up as a material before it ever turns up as ink.

New in this set: the aura. Five thin rings around the mark, one of them red. It came straight out of the tagline and ended up on the poster, the till roll and the last slide of this series.

The alphabet

Black — the night set · post-03-the-alphabet

The night set. A is for Aura, and also for the chair.

The shopfront is the identity as a single object — red board, cream mark, one ink, one chair in the window. Nothing else needed painting.

Take the red away and the frame is still a letter. That half does the quiet work on the wrapping paper, the shelf edge and the back of every card. Six chairs, A/01 to A/06, and by the end the code brands more surfaces than the logo does.

03

In print

Paper — the workbench set · post-04-in-print

The workbench set — everything that goes to the printer, flat, on paper white.

Swing tag, A2 poster, business card, gummed tape, seals. One ink wherever one ink will do. The tape is the newest piece: mark and wordmark repeating down a 50 mm roll, and suddenly every parcel that leaves the shop is a moving poster.

The fascia photo is the same artwork at architectural scale — cream vinyl on a painted board, and it has to read at forty metres.

04

The system

Red — the loud set · post-02-the-system

The rulebook, printed on its own accent colour for once.

Cream 62, red 26, black 12 — written down so a print shop never has to guess and the feed never lands two reds side by side. Two typefaces, five sizes, one permitted angle: the 32° of the chair back. It cuts covers, wipes between slides, sets the corner of the swing tag, and nothing else gets to be diagonal.

The app icon is the stress case. Under 48 pixels the wordmark comes off and the chair carries everything.

05

The close

All three grounds in turn · post-05-the-close

Last set for Aura Seat, and each slide takes a different ground — cream, black, red, taking turns.

The umbrella is the hardest test the mark ever faces: one ink, wet fabric, moving. If it survives that, a shopfront is easy.

What closes the project is the paper. The one-line note that rides with every delivery, the till roll, and the aura rings one more time — this time on black. Thanks for following this one.
