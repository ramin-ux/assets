Aura Seat is a concept store in London that sells chairs and nothing else.

The identity is one move: the frame of the chair is the letter A. Black frame, red seat, and neither half works alone. The card is where it gets physical — letterpressed into thick cotton stock with the cut edge painted red, so the accent colour turns up as a material before it ever turns up as ink.

New in this set: the aura. Five thin rings around the mark, one of them red. It came straight out of the tagline and ended up on the poster, the till roll and the last slide of this series.
