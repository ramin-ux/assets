The rulebook, printed on its own accent colour for once.

Cream 62, red 26, black 12 — written down so a print shop never has to guess and the feed never lands two reds side by side. Two typefaces, five sizes, one permitted angle: the 32° of the chair back. It cuts covers, wipes between slides, sets the corner of the swing tag, and nothing else gets to be diagonal.

The app icon is the stress case. Under 48 pixels the wordmark comes off and the chair carries everything.
