The workbench set — everything that goes to the printer, flat, on paper white.

Swing tag, A2 poster, business card, gummed tape, seals. One ink wherever one ink will do. The tape is the newest piece: mark and wordmark repeating down a 50 mm roll, and suddenly every parcel that leaves the shop is a moving poster.

The fascia photo is the same artwork at architectural scale — cream vinyl on a painted board, and it has to read at forty metres.
