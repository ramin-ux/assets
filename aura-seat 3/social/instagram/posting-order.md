## Rules of the series

- Slide 1 is always the mark alone. Slide 8 is always the lockup. Slide 2 is always the photograph.
- Each post keeps one colour mood; only `the close` is allowed to rotate grounds.
- Photographs carry cream chrome, a red code chip and one hairline foot rule. Nothing else goes on an image.
- Never two red covers in a row in the feed.
- Hashtags go in the **first comment**, never in the caption. No emoji, no dates.
- No address, phone, names or URL anywhere — the store has not opened yet. "Opening soon" is the only contact line.
- Where a video is listed, upload the MP4 in place of that image slide.
