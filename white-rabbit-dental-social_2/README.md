## How a carousel is built

Slide 01 is the mark on a flat ground, slide 10 is the lockup. In between, five photographs
and three graphic pages alternate. A slide is either a photograph at full bleed or a pure
graphic page. Nothing is ever drawn on top of a photograph and no photograph is used twice
inside one carousel.

## Specs

- Slides: 1080 x 1350, sRGB, JPG quality 93, 10 per carousel, 5 carousels, 50 files.
- Collages: 1280 x 1104. Three PNG, three MP4.
- Video: H.264, yuv420p, 60 fps, 5 s, no audio, three animations in each.

## Type

Inter Display for headlines and specimens, Inter for labels and captions.
